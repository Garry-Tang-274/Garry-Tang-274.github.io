    <h1>Photography<br>摄影作品</h1>
    <p class="lead-cn">这些照片并不按地点陈列，而是围绕一个反复出现的关系：人在环境中的位置，以及道路、建筑、雨水、山河和季节如何为个体建立尺度。</p>
    <p>The photographs are not organized primarily by location. They return to one relationship: the position of an individual within an environment, and the way roads, architecture, rain, landscapes, and seasons establish scale.</p>

  <section class="gallery-notes">
    <h2>公开版本与隐私 · Public Copies and Privacy</h2>
    <p class="cn">所有公开展示图均重新编码，已经移除 EXIF、GPS 与其他嵌入元数据。可识别的车牌、电话号码和联系方式经过不可逆局部模糊；原始高分辨率文件不随网站公开。</p>
    <p>Every public display copy has been re-encoded with EXIF, GPS, and other embedded metadata removed. Identifiable license plates, phone numbers, and contact details are irreversibly blurred; original high-resolution files are not published with the site.</p>
    <p class="cn">除必要脱敏外，未使用生成式重绘，也未改变照片的主体位置、画幅关系和整体色彩。</p>
    <p>Apart from necessary redaction, no generative redrawing was used, and subject placement, aspect relationships, and overall color were preserved.</p>
